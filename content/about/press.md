I am an amateur astronomer, telescope maker and science writer in Tucson, Arizona. I started building telescopes at 12, ground my first mirror at 14, and have owned more than 500 telescopes since.

## What I do

- **Write about telescopes.** I have been Lead Writer and Editor at [TelescopicWatch](https://telescopicwatch.com/) since 2018, and I write reviews and buying guides for [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/author/zane-landers).
- **Build them.** My main telescope is a [14.7" f/2.9 Dobsonian](/projects/147-inch-f29/) that I built myself. I am rebuilding a [24" f/4](/projects/24-inch-f4/), and I helped design the 3D-printed Hadley 114.
- **Show people the sky.** I host private [star parties](/astronomy/starparties/) in Tucson, Phoenix and across the Southwest, and set up telescopes on the sidewalk for free. See [sidewalk astronomy](/astronomy/sidewalk-astronomy/).
- **Work on dark skies.** I am a founding board member of [DarkSky Southern Arizona](https://darkskysoaz.org/). More on [protecting dark skies](/astronomy/dark-skies/).
- **Help other observers.** I help moderate [r/telescopes](https://www.reddit.com/r/telescopes/), and I point people with questions to the [Observational Astronomy Discord](https://discord.gg/astronomy).

I have a Master of Science in Marketing from the University of Arizona and a Bachelor of Arts in Communications from the University of Connecticut. My [resume](/resume/) is here.

## Book me to speak

I am available to speak to astronomy clubs, star parties, conferences and other groups, in person or remotely. Topics I cover:

- **Telescope making.** Building large, fast Dobsonians, thin mirrors and spray silvering.
- **Night vision astronomy.** What an image intensifier shows at the eyepiece, and what it does not. See [night vision astronomy explained](/astronomy/night-vision/).
- **Public outreach.** Sidewalk astronomy, running star parties, and getting people who have never looked through a telescope to do it.
- **Smart telescopes.** I have owned and reviewed nearly every one on the market, and I talk about them from a visual observer's point of view.

To set something up, use the [contact page](/links/contact/) and tell me the date, the audience and how long you want me to speak.

## For journalists

I am happy to talk about telescopes, buying advice, amateur astronomy, light pollution and what is visible in the sky. The [contact page](/links/contact/) is the fastest way to reach me.
