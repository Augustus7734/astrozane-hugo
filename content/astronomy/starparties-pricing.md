## What the price covers

- Hand-built telescopes, binoculars to pass around and a night vision device.
- Me as your guide for the whole night.
- Travel within the Tucson and Phoenix areas.
- Rescheduling at no charge if it is cloudy.

The final price depends on the date, the location, the size of the group and anything extra you would like. A deposit is required to hold a date.
