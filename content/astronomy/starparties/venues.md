If you run a resort, hotel, guest ranch, vineyard or event venue in Arizona, a star party is an easy evening to add. I bring the telescopes and run the night. You don't have to buy or learn anything.

I have run a Saturday-evening stargazing series at [Mount Lemmon Lodge](https://mountlemmonlodge.com/), star parties for guests at [White Stallion Ranch](https://www.whitestallion.com/), a stargazing and sound bath evening at [Tucson Mountain Guest Ranch](https://tucsonmountainguestranch.com/), and a stargazing event at [El Conquistador Tucson, A Hilton Resort](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/tushthh-el-conquistador-tucson/), along with private events all over Southern Arizona. Venue dates are open for **January through May 2027 and August through December 2027**.

## What your guests get

- A look through the eyepiece at the Moon, planets, star clusters, nebulae and galaxies, picked for what is best that night.
- My 14.7" hand-built telescope with seated viewing, a second telescope, binoculars to pass around, and a night vision device that shows nebulae live.
- No screens, and no experience needed. It works for all ages.
- A printed handout on what they saw, to take home.
- A short talk before viewing, if you want a longer program.

## Ways to set it up

| Option | How it works | Base price |
|---|---|---|
| Flat rate | You pay a set fee and offer the evening however you like: free for guests, ticketed, or part of a package. | From $400 for a 2-hour evening, up to 40 guests |
| Recurring series | Four or more nights booked for the season on a regular schedule. | From $250 a night |
| Ticketed | No cost to you up front. We both promote it and split ticket sales 50/50. Tickets usually run $45 to $65. | $200 minimum to me per night |
| Guest add-on | You offer private star parties to overnight guests or event clients at my regular rates, and keep 15% of each booking you send. | From $300 per group |
| Weddings and corporate events | A private evening for your client's group, scheduled around dinner or the reception. | From $500 for 2 hours |
| Larger events | For 40 to 100 guests I bring more telescopes, and the evening runs up to 3 hours. | From $700 for 40 to 70 guests, from $999 for 70 to 100 |

These are base estimates for the Tucson area and depend on the date, location, group size and extras. Venues around Sonoita, Elgin, Sierra Vista and Willcox add a $75 travel fee. Phoenix and Scottsdale venues start at $500. A deposit holds each date.

From 2027 my 24" telescope is available too, at a higher rate. It can come by itself or alongside the 14.7".

## What I need from you

- A reasonably flat spot with a view of the sky and parking nearby.
- Outdoor lights near the telescopes switched off or dimmed once we start.
- The address or a map pin ahead of time, so I can check the horizon and the lighting.
- Someone I can reach on the night.

## What I handle

- All the equipment, setup and takedown.
- Promotion. I can send you an event description, photos and social posts to use.
- The weather. I watch the forecast in the days before, and cloudy nights are rescheduled at no charge.

## Best nights in 2027

These are the Fridays and Saturdays I would pick. Weeknights are open too.

| | Dark evenings, for the Milky Way, nebulae and galaxies | Moon nights, best for big, casual crowds |
|---|---|---|
| January to May | January 8-9 and 29-30, February 5-6 and 26-27, March 5-6 and 26-27, April 2-3 and 9-10, May 7-8 and 28-29 | January 15-16, February 12-13, March 12-13, April 16-17, May 14-15 |
| August to December | August 27-28, September 3-4 and 24-25, October 1-2, 22-23 and 29-30, November 19-20 and 26-27, December 17-18 | August 13-14, September 10-11, October 8-9, November 5-6, December 3-4 |

Jupiter and Mars are both at their best in February and stay well placed through April. The Orion Nebula is up all winter and into early April. In August and September the summer Milky Way is high after dark, and Saturn is at its best from October through December.

**Summer.** August and September are monsoon season, so pick a backup date when you book. June and July are by request only: it isn't fully dark until after 9 pm, and storms cancel a lot of nights.
