I fix telescopes and help people get theirs working, in person, in the Tucson and Phoenix areas.

I have been building and repairing telescopes since 2016 and have owned more than 500 of them. I also review them for [TelescopicWatch](https://telescopicwatch.com/), so odds are I have used yours or something close to it.

## What I can help with

- **Collimation.** Lining up the optics of a Newtonian, Dobsonian or Schmidt-Cassegrain, and showing you how to do it yourself.
- **Setup and a first night out.** Putting a new telescope together properly and learning to find things with it.
- **Working out what is wrong.** Blurry views, a GoTo mount that won't align, a mount that wobbles or sticks.
- **Repairs and adjustments.** Focusers, mirror cells, mounts and finders.
- **Cleaning optics.** Mirrors and lenses, done safely.
- **Restoring older telescopes.** Some of my own [restoration projects](/projects/restoration-projects/) are here.
- **A second opinion before you buy**, new or used.
- **Gift telescopes.** Setting up one you were given, at your house, and showing you how to use it.
- **Computerized and smart telescopes.** GoTo mounts that will not align, and smart telescopes that will not connect or focus.
- **Old and inherited telescopes.** Working out what you have and what it is worth.

## How it works

Use the [contact page](/links/contact/) to send me the make and model, what it is doing, and a photo if you can. I will tell you whether I can fix it and give you a price before I start.

Some telescopes are not worth fixing. If yours is one of them, I will say so.
